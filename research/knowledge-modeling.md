# Knowledge Modeling

[← Profile](../README.md)

**Organizing bilingual terminology into a structured knowledge base.**

A personal exploration of Chinese–Japanese terminology, combining Python-based collection, RDF relation modeling, and an ontology prototype built with Protégé and OntoGraf.

The focus was on making concepts and their relationships explicit: aligning bilingual expressions, organizing terminology into categories, and representing how terms relate to one another.

## Approach

| Stage | Technical work |
| --- | --- |
| Collection | Gather terminology and explanations from a public bilingual reference source using Python. |
| Normalization | Clean terms, align bilingual expressions, and organize concepts. |
| Representation | Model classes, properties, and subject–predicate–object relations with RDF. |
| Exploration | Inspect the ontology and visualize concept relations in Protégé and OntoGraf. |

## Outcome

A bilingual ontology prototype with defined concept classes, properties, and semantic relations, supported by a graph view for exploring the terminology.

**Tools:** Python · RDF · Protégé · OntoGraf
