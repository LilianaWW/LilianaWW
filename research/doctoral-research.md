# Doctoral Research Performance

[← Profile](../README.md)

**Inequality of Doctoral Research Performance—Evidence from the Field of Education in China**<br>
**Guoli Wang**, Haoran Tian<br>
Research poster selected for **Tsinghua University's Higher Education Forum**

An empirical study of publication inequality using CNKI records covering 6,189 doctoral students in education, 2015–2024.

| Focus | Approach |
| --- | --- |
| Data construction | Python-based collection and construction of publication measures. |
| Distribution | Gini coefficients and coefficients of variation. |
| Statistical modeling | Negative binomial regression for publication counts; OLS for journal impact-factor measures. |
| Comparisons | Institutional background, funding, and collaboration structure. |

Journal impact factors serve as a proxy in this study, rather than a complete measure of an individual's research quality.

*This page summarizes the coauthored research poster. It does not redistribute underlying research data.*
