### 人均规模趋同与质量分化：省际高等教育资源集聚分布的双重效应研究

田浩然、**王国郦**、郭峻仙<br>
*教育发展研究* · **CSSCI**

The study examines how higher education resources are distributed across 31 Chinese provinces from 2005 to 2022, distinguishing enrollment scale from resource measures such as assets and faculty.

| Focus | Approach |
| --- | --- |
| Panel modeling | Province and year fixed effects; growth-rate outcomes; economic and demographic controls. |
| Regional variation | Geographic and economic subgroup comparisons. |
| Robustness | Lagged explanatory variables, Driscoll–Kraay standard errors, and alternative measures. |

**Finding:** convergence in per-capita enrollment scale can coexist with divergence in selected resource measures, with differences across regions.

These are observational estimates; robustness checks assess sensitivity and do not by themselves establish causality.
